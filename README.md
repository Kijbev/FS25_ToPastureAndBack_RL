# To Pasture And Back, RL Edition

To Pasture And Back takes a herd from a barn out to a field or pasture and brings it home again. This edition is built for [Realistic Livestock RM](https://github.com/rittermod/FS25_RealisticLivestockRM): on the pasture every animal is the same individual you own in the barn, with its breed, age, sex and ear tags. Feeding, breeding and production stay in the barn.

Made by Kijbev, a husband and wife team. Website: [kijbev-modding.com](https://kijbev-modding.com/mods/to-pasture-and-back-rl)

## Requirements

- Farming Simulator 25, patch 1.25.1 or newer.
- [Realistic Livestock RM](https://github.com/rittermod/FS25_RealisticLivestockRM) by Ritter (`FS25_RealisticLivestockRM`). This edition does nothing without it.
- The plain To Pasture And Back from ModHub switches itself off while Realistic Livestock RM is loaded, so only this edition runs. You do not need to remove it.

## Installation

1. Download `FS25_ToPastureAndBack_RL.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/Kijbev/FS25_ToPastureAndBack_RL/releases/latest).
2. Put the zip, without unpacking it, in `Documents\My Games\FarmingSimulator2025\mods`.
3. Activate it for your savegame, together with Realistic Livestock RM.

In multiplayer, every player needs the same zip.

## Features

- Right Ctrl + J picks up the herd at a barn, or a herd out on a field on the farmland you are standing on, to move it to another field.
- Right Ctrl + G drops the herd on the field you are standing on.
- Several herds can be picked up and dropped one per key press, on one field or on several fields. Pigs are never picked up together with other species.
- Each species goes only on ground that suits it: cows, horses and alpacas on standing grass, sheep and goats on grass of any height, pigs on mowed fields, stubble, fallow, plowed or cultivated ground. Growing crops are off limits for all of them.
- Animals too young to leave stay in the barn while the rest of the herd is out.
- A field without a fence gets a decorative temporary fence with no collision, which can be switched off.
- Right Ctrl + K brings home the herd on the farmland you are standing on, or every herd of your farm when you are elsewhere. By default a second press confirms it.
- Right Ctrl + N cancels the pickup, and every herd you are transporting goes back to where it came from.
- Right Ctrl + L shows which herds are out and where.
- An optional curfew brings every herd home at 10 PM. An optional reminder at 6 PM says when herds are still out.
- Each player sets how many birds a chicken coop shows, up to Realistic Livestock RM's own number of visible animals.
- Settings for message size, color, background and position, and the warning style.
- Right Ctrl + F12 opens the Kijbev help panel with the keys of every active Kijbev mod.
- Herd messages show to the players of the barn's farm.

Herds out on a field: cows, water buffalo, sheep, goats, pigs, horses and alpacas. Chickens stay in their coop.

Every key can be changed in the game's controls menu. The settings are in the game's settings menu.

## Problems

Email kijbev-modding@proton.me with a few words about what happened, and attach your game log from `Documents\My Games\FarmingSimulator2025\log.txt`. The game overwrites the log every time it starts, so copy it before you launch the game again.
